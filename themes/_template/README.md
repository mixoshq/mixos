One or two sentences on what this theme looks like and who it is for. Assume the
reader is scanning a catalogue of themes and has not seen it yet.

## What it looks like

The palette in words — what is dark, what carries the accent, how loud it is.
Link a screenshot with an absolute URL; a theme is the one extension where a
picture genuinely does the work.

## Where it came from

A brand book, a well-known palette, an existing product. Worth saying if the
colours are deliberately someone's — it tells a reader whether it will fit
alongside their own branding or fight it.

## What it ships

Fonts it bundles, assets it replaces (logo, favicon, app icons), and which
`.mix-*` surfaces it styles beyond the base tokens.

## Worth knowing

Whether there is a matching light or dark counterpart, anything it deliberately
leaves alone, and any licence constraint on the fonts or brand assets it carries.
