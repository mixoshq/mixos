# MixOS Trademark Policy

The MixOS code is open source under MPL-2.0. **The MixOS name and logo are
not.** A software licence grants copyright and patent rights, never trademark
rights, so taking the code doesn't come with the right to call what you made
"MixOS".

The point of this policy isn't to stop anyone building on MixOS. It's to make
sure that when someone sees the name, they can trust it means the real project.
Forks, extensions, hosting and commercial use are all welcome. They just need
names of their own.

The marks covered here are the name **MixOS**, the MixOS logo and wordmark, and
any confusingly similar name or design. They are owned by
**Tangent Thinking, LLC** ("we").

## Fine without asking

- **Talking about MixOS.** Articles, tutorials, talks, videos, books, courses,
  reviews and comparisons can use the name freely, as long as they don't
  suggest we wrote or endorsed them.
- **Saying what your work is for.** You can describe an app, connector, theme or
  integration as "for MixOS", "works with MixOS" or "built on MixOS". For
  example, *Timesheets for MixOS* is fine.
- **Redistributing unmodified MixOS.** If you ship an official release unchanged,
  you can call it MixOS.
- **Saying where a fork came from.** A fork with its own name can say it's
  "based on MixOS" or "a fork of MixOS".
- **Offering MixOS hosting.** You can say you host, install, manage or support
  MixOS. For example, *Acme Cloud — managed hosting for MixOS* is fine.
- **Linking to the project with the logo.** You can use the unmodified logo to
  link to MixOS or the official repository.

## Not allowed without written permission

- **Naming a product, service, company or extension "MixOS"**, or anything that
  starts with or contains it as the brand, such as *MixOS Pro*, *MixOS Cloud*,
  *MixOS CRM* or *MixHost*-style blends that read as MixOS. Put the MixOS
  reference after your own name: *Acme for MixOS*, not *MixOS Acme*.
- **Calling a modified version "MixOS".** Once you've changed the code and
  you distribute it or run it for others, it needs a different name. Rename it,
  replace the logo, and say "based on MixOS" if you like.
- **Using MixOS in a domain name**, app-store listing name or social media
  handle, such as `mixoshosting.com` or `@mixos_apps`.
- **Implying that we endorse, sponsor, certify or partner with you** when we
  don't. That includes calling a hosting service or extension "official",
  "certified" or "approved".
- **Modifying the logo**, combining it with your own logo, or using it as
  your own product's icon.
- **Using the marks on merchandise** you sell.

## Forks

Forking is a right the licence guarantees, and we'd rather a fork be easy to
tell apart than discouraged. If you publish a fork:

1. Pick a name that isn't MixOS or confusingly similar.
2. Replace the MixOS logo and wordmark in the interface, the favicon and the
   documentation.
3. Say "based on MixOS" if you want people to know where it came from.

Running a modified copy privately inside your own organisation doesn't need any
of this.

## Asking for permission

If you want to do something this page doesn't allow, or you aren't sure whether
it does, ask before launching: **[legal@mixos.org](mailto:legal@mixos.org)**. Tell
us the name, what it's for, and where it will appear.

We may update this policy. If something you were already doing becomes
disallowed, we'll give reasonable notice before expecting a change.

*This policy explains how we'll handle the marks. It isn't legal advice, and it
doesn't limit any right you have under trademark law, such as fair use.*
